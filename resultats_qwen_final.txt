### Explanation of Multi-Head Attention:
Multi-head attention consists of several attention layers running in parallel. In each attention layer, the model computes attention scores for each pair of keys and values. The scores are then normalized using a softmax function to produce attention weights. These weights are used to compute the final values by taking a weighted sum of the keys. This process is repeated for multiple attention heads, allowing the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces at different positions. With a single attention head, averaging the attention scores can inhibit this joint attention. The scaled dot-product attention mechanism is used to compute the attention scores, where each key is divided by the square root of its dimension before the dot product is computed.
